# Board meeting, 14 January 2025

Present: the chair (Helga Einarsdóttir), the CEO, the CFO, two investor directors and the CTO as a guest.

## 1. Results 2024

Revenue for 2024 was ISK 798 million against a budget of ISK 760 million. EBITDA was ISK 91 million, an 11.4% margin. Cash at year end: ISK 240 million. There is no bank debt apart from the equipment lease.

## 2. Customer base

The company ended the year with 214 active operators, up from 171. Net revenue retention was 112%. Three accounts above ISK 20 million a year each represent 19% of revenue; the board asked management to present a concentration-risk plan before the autumn.

## 3. Strategy: no marketplace

Management presented a paper on building a consumer marketplace on top of Skerry. After discussion the board decided not to pursue it in 2025. The main objections were channel conflict with our customers, the marketing spend needed to attract travellers, and the distraction from reliability work. Revisit in 2026.

## 4. Office

The lease at Hólmaslóð expires on 31 May 2025. The board approved a five-year lease on new premises at Fiskislóð 31 at ISK 4.1 million per month, with a move date of 1 June 2025. Fit-out budget capped at ISK 28 million.

## 5. Security and compliance

The CTO reported on the annual penetration test: no critical findings, 4 medium findings, all closed within 30 days. ISO 27001 certification is deferred to 2026 given the cost. The board asked for quarterly updates on SEV-2 and SEV-1 incidents.

## 6. People

Headcount is 40 and the plan is 46 by year end. The board approved a stock option pool increase of 1.5 percentage points. Employee satisfaction survey result: 8.1 out of 10 and an eNPS of 52.

## 7. Risks

- Dependence on one payment provider, Paylink; a secondary provider will be evaluated in Q2.
- Currency exposure; the board approved hedging up to 50% of forecast euro revenue with forward contracts.
- Key-person risk in the pricing squad.

## 8. Any other business

The chair thanked staff for a strong year. The next meeting is on 22 April 2025. Minutes approved at the end of the meeting.

## 10. Decisions recorded

1. Approve the 2025 budget as presented in December.
2. Approve the lease for Fiskislóð 31.
3. Do not pursue a consumer marketplace in 2025.
4. Approve hedging policy of up to 50% of forecast euro revenue.
5. Approve the option pool increase.
6. Appoint the CFO as owner of the concentration-risk plan, due before the autumn board meeting.
