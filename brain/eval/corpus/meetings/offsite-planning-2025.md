# Offsite 2025 planning

Location: Akureyri. Dates: 12 to 14 June 2025. Budget: ISK 1.8 million in total for 44 people, which works out to roughly ISK 40,900 each, covering the bus, two nights at a guesthouse and one group dinner. Flights are not included; the Reykjavik group drives in two minibuses.

The committee (Hildur, Sunna, Arnar) booked a whale-watching trip from Hauganes and a Saturday-evening pub quiz. Day two is reserved for the all-hands and a strategy workshop. Vegetarian and vegan meals are arranged; send dietary needs to Hildur by 15 May.
