# Budget planning, 2 December 2024

Present: Björn, Sigríður, Guðmundur, Þórunn, Davíð.

We agreed the headline numbers that go into the 2025 budget: revenue target ISK 980 million, headcount growth from 40 to 46, and an infrastructure budget of ISK 54 million. Marketing stays flat. Björn pushed for a 3% contingency line; agreed. Mobile app funding was debated; Þórunn argued the pilot customers justify full funding, Guðmundur wanted it staged. Final: fund it fully but with a stage gate after the beta.
