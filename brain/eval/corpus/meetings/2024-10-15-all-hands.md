# All-hands, 15 October 2024

Notes by Ragnheiður. About 38 people attended in the office and 6 over video.

## CEO update

The company closed the third quarter with revenue of ISK 212 million, 14% above plan and 31% above the same quarter last year. The summer peak was the busiest ever: 41,200 bookings in July. The CEO thanked the support team for keeping first-response time under 4 hours throughout.

## Product

Þórunn presented the 2025 themes: reliability, the mobile app and the database migration. The group asked whether Skerry would ever sell to consumers directly; the answer was no, for the reasons recorded in the strategy paper. A demo of the new Geysir last-minute discount drew applause and one question about whether operators can switch it off (they can).

## Engineering

Guðmundur reported that availability in September was 99.96%. The one incident was a 12-minute degradation of the notification worker. He announced that two engineers will join in November and that the hiring bar for senior backend engineers will include a 90-minute pairing session rather than a take-home test.

## People

Ragnheiður announced the new education grant of ISK 150,000 per person per year, effective 1 January. The company will also pay for one team lunch per quarter per team up to ISK 6,000 per head. The annual review cycle will move from April to May and November. Staff turnover over the past 12 months was 7%, down from 11%.

## Finance

Björn explained that the euro strengthened against the krona by 4% this year, which helps revenue since about 55% of our sales are in euro, but raises the cost of cloud hosting. Hedging policy is under review.

## Offsite

The next offsite will be in Akureyri in June. Committee: Hildur, Sunna, Arnar. A poll will decide between hiking and a boat trip.

## Questions

Q: Will the office move? A: The lease at Hólmaslóð ends in May 2025; options are being evaluated and a decision will be taken by the board in January.
Q: Are we getting a dog? A: See the animals policy.
Q: Can we get standing desks? A: Facilities will trial six.

Next all-hands: 28 January 2025.

## Lightning talks

Sunna gave a five-minute talk on how the cache-age alert caught a supplier feed problem before any customer noticed; she showed the graph and said the threshold of five minutes was chosen after replaying six months of traffic. Kári presented the preview-environment cost report: preview environments are 11% of the cloud bill and about a third are never opened by anyone, so he proposed shortening their lifetime. Hildur showed the new support dashboard, which now splits tickets by language, and pointed out that German tickets are growing at 15% a month.

## Kudos

Elín thanked Sólrún for handling a difficult weekend incident calmly. Davíð thanked everyone who had helped to carry boxes during the storage room clear-out. Björn gave a bottle of Icelandic gin to the team who found an invoice error worth ISK 1.2 million.

## Housekeeping

The coffee machine will be serviced on 22 October. The kitchen rota is being retired. The staff association's autumn soup night is on 25 October and everyone is encouraged to bring a pot.
