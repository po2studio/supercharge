## Context

Tour operators who sign up for the booking platform asked for time to import their tours and invite staff before paying.

## Decision

Every new operator gets a free trial of 14 days, with the full feature set.

## Alternatives

No trial, with a refund guarantee instead. Rejected: it moves the risk to us and delays revenue accounting.

## Consequences

The sales team sends the reminder to add a payment method on day 10.
