## Context

Support escalated almost every refund request to a team lead, and the answers took days.

## Decision

Support approves a refund on its own up to ISK 50,000 per request. Above that it goes to a team lead. This replaces the limit written in the support playbook.

## Consequences

The playbook still shows the old number until someone edits it; this decision is the rule.
