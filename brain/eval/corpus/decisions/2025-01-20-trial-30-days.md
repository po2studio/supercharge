## Context

Half of the operators who left in the first quarter said that 14 days was not enough to run one real booking season week through the platform.

## Decision

Every new operator gets a free trial of 30 days, with the full feature set. The 14-day trial no longer applies.

## Alternatives

Keeping 14 days and letting sales extend it by hand. Rejected: nobody tracked the extensions.

## Consequences

The reminder to add a payment method moves to day 24.
