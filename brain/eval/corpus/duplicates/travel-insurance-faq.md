# Travel insurance FAQ

Every guest who books a Hrafn tour can add travel insurance at checkout. These are the questions support hears most often.

## What does the insurance cover?

The insurance covers cancellation for illness, delayed or lost luggage up to ISK 150,000, and medical costs abroad up to ISK 5 million. It does not cover cancellations made because of a change of mind.

## How do guests claim?

Guests claim through the insurer's own website within 30 days of returning home, with their Hrafn booking number and receipts. Hrafn support cannot approve claims but can confirm that a booking existed.

## Can insurance be added after booking?

Yes, until 24 hours before departure, from the booking page. The price is then 6 percent of the booking value.
