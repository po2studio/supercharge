Beta feedback digest, week of 18 November, collected from the six guesthouse owners in the Skarfur widget pilot: Sólveig from Hof said the widget loaded in under a second on her mobile connection and that she was delighted, but the calendar start day defaulted to Sunday which confused her guests, who expect Monday. Ari at the Seyðisfjörður hostel asked for a way to hide the child-price line because he does not offer family rooms. Three owners asked for dark mode. One owner, Halldóra, reported that the confirmation email landed in spam for Hotmail addresses; we checked the SPF record and it was missing the second mail provider, which Kári fixed the same day. The average satisfaction score on our five-point scale was 4.3. Requests that came up more than once: a printable booking sheet, Facebook page embedding and cash-on-arrival as a payment option. We decided not to build cash-on-arrival because it breaks the Paylink flow.
