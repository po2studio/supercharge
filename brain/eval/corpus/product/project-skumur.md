# Project Skúmur

Skúmur is the mobile app for operators: check today's departures, scan guest QR codes and see payouts. Built with React Native for iOS and Android. Product owner is Þórunn Kristjánsdóttir. Closed beta starts 3 March 2025 with eight pilot customers, one of which is Hveravellir Hot Springs. General availability is targeted for September 2025.
