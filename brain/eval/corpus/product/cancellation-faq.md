# Customer FAQ: cancellations

**Can a traveller cancel?** Yes. Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before departure. Between 48 and 24 hours before departure, a 50% fee applies. Inside 24 hours the booking is non-refundable unless the operator cancels or weather closes the route.

**How fast is the refund?** Refunds reach the card within 5 to 7 banking days.

**Can the operator change the rules?** Operators may set stricter windows for private tours, but never shorter than 24 hours.
