# Sick leave

Staff who are unwell for up to two consecutive working days need only notify their team lead before 09:00 on the first day. From the third day a physician's attestation is required and must be sent to the People team.

Full pay continues for the statutory period according to length of service: 1 month after one year of service, up to 3 months after ten years. Please do not come into the office while contagious; you can work from home if you feel fit enough.
