# Business trips: booking and limits

All business trips need written approval from your line manager at least 10 days before departure. Approvals are recorded in the Trips board in the intranet.

## Flights
Economy class for any flight shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed only on flights longer than 6 hours and only with CFO approval. Book through our own Skerry corporate account so the carbon figures end up in the sustainability report.

## Accommodation
Hotels in Reykjavik are capped at ISK 22,000 per night. Lodging outside Iceland is capped at EUR 160 per night, breakfast included where possible. Private rentals are fine if they are cheaper than the hotel cap.

## After the trip
File the expense report within 14 days of returning. Attach hotel invoices; boarding passes are not needed.
