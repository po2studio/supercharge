# Cycle-to-work grant

Staff who commute on two wheels, pedal-powered or assisted, can reimburse up to ISK 45,000 per calendar year of equipment and repairs: helmets, lights, tyres, servicing. Claims go through Payday with the shop receipt.

There are covered racks behind the building and a shower in the basement next to the server cupboard. Winter studs are covered by the grant too.
