# Whistleblowing procedure

If you suspect fraud, safety violations or serious breaches of law, you can report in confidence by emailing whistle@hrafn.example, which is read only by Ragnheiður Sigurðardóttir and the chair of the board. Anonymous reports are accepted through the web form in the intranet.

We acknowledge every report within 3 working days and give you feedback on what has been done within 3 months. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is prohibited and will itself be treated as a serious breach.
