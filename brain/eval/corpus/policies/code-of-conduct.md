# Code of conduct

We are about 40 people; most of us eat lunch together. Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are grounds for dismissal.

Disagree in the open, decide in writing. Do not share customer data in public channels. Gifts from suppliers above ISK 15,000 in value must be declared to your manager.

Conflicts of interest, such as a side business selling travel services, must be disclosed to the CFO before you start. Breaches are handled by the People team under the disciplinary process.
