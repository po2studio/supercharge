# Parental leave

Each parent is entitled to six months of leave under Icelandic law, paid by the state benefit fund up to the statutory ceiling.

During the first three months of parental leave Hrafn tops up the state benefit so that the employee receives 100% of base salary. Months four to six are paid by the fund alone. Leave may be taken in blocks until the child turns 24 months. Tell the People team at least 6 weeks before the planned start. Time on leave counts toward service for seniority and holiday accrual.
