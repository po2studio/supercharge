# Holiday and annual leave

Full-time staff receive 27 days of paid annual leave per leave year (1 May to 30 April), which is three more than the statutory minimum of 24. Part-time staff accrue pro rata.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next leave year but must be taken by 31 May; anything above that lapses unless the employee was prevented from taking leave by the company.

Holidays of more than 15 consecutive working days need your manager's agreement 8 weeks in advance. Public holidays do not count against your leave. If you fall ill during leave and obtain a medical attestation, those days are credited back.
