# Home-office allowance

Every employee gets a one-off ISK 60,000 grant towards a desk, chair or monitor arm, claimable once per employment, within the first 90 days. Receipts must be attached in Payday.

In addition Hrafn contributes ISK 5,000 each month towards your home internet connection; it is added to your payslip automatically if you work remotely at least one day a week.

Equipment bought with the grant stays yours. Laptops and phones remain company property.
