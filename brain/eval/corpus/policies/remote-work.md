# Remote work policy

Everyone may do up to three days a week from home. The remaining days are office days, normally Tuesday and Thursday, so that teams overlap.

On home-office days you must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 Icelandic time. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours where possible.

Staying outside Iceland while on duty is capped at 20 days per calendar year and needs a heads-up to the People team at least two weeks before, because of tax and insurance rules. Employees may not use public wifi without the VPN. See the home-office allowance policy for equipment funding.
