# Everyday expenses policy

Hrafn reimburses reasonable costs incurred while doing your job. For meals during a normal working day in Iceland, the limit is ISK 4,500 per meal; client dinners may go up to ISK 9,000 per head, and the most senior attendee signs off.

On trips outside Iceland, meals are reimbursed at a flat EUR 45 per day, no receipts needed for the flat rate.

Submit claims in Payday within 14 days of the purchase. Claims older than 60 days are not paid unless the CFO makes an exception. Alcohol is never reimbursed, with the single exception of the annual customer dinner in October.
