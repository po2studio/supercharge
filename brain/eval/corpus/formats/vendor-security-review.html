Vendor security review 2024

Each year an outside firm tests the parts of Hrafn that guests and operators can reach from the internet.

Checkout API

Brimvörn Security carried out the penetration test of the checkout API in week 42 of 2024. They reported three medium findings and no critical ones. All three medium findings were fixed by 8 November.

Operator portal

The operator portal is tested in spring. Its last report listed one low finding, an overly verbose error message, which was removed.
