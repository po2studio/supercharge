Call with Fjallasýn Tours, 10 March 2025
Present: Karítas Jónsdóttir, Davíð Árnason, Helga from Fjallasýn

Davíð: Thanks for making time, Helga. We wanted to go through the summer shuttle arrangements.
Helga: Sure. Our main question is group sizes. Some of our guests book in twos and threes.
Karítas: The airport shuttle is guaranteed from six guests. Below that the guests share a seat on the scheduled bus, and we cannot promise a private vehicle.
Helga: Six is a lot for us. Can the minimum be lowered in the shoulder season?
Davíð: We can look at that for May and September. It would need approval from the operations lead.
Karítas: I would rather keep six for July and August, when the vehicles are fully booked anyway.
Helga: That is fair. Please send the proposal in writing.
Davíð: We will send it on Friday.
