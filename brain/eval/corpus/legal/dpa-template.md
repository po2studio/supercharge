# Data processing agreement: summary

Hrafn acts as processor for guest data entered by operators. Our standard DPA lists sub-processors (cloud host in Frankfurt, two email providers, Paylink for payments), commits us to notify operators of a breach within 48 hours, and allows one audit per year with 30 days' notice. Operators can download the signed DPA from the console. Changes to sub-processors are announced 30 days ahead.
