# Refund exceptions

Outside the cancellation windows, a refund can be given if: weather closed the route (full refund), the operator cancelled (full refund), or a guest was medically unable to travel with an attestation (80% refund). Goodwill refunds above ISK 30,000 need the Head of Support; above ISK 150,000 the CFO. Log each exception in the refund sheet with the booking reference.
