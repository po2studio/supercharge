# Nordic Wanderers: account notes

Agency selling self-drive trips in Iceland to Germans and Dutch, 9 staff. Signed in June 2023. Contract grants them an SLA of 99.9% monthly availability with a 5% service credit for each full 0.1% below target. Payment terms are net 45 rather than our standard net 30, which was agreed during negotiation.

Primary contact: Elín Haraldsdóttir, managing director. Technical contact: Jan de Vries, who prefers email over phone.

Their biggest wish is multi-currency carts, which is on the Q2 roadmap.
