# Support playbook

Support hours are 08:00 to 18:00 on working days. First-response targets:

- P1 (production down or payments failing): 30 minutes, around the clock via the on-call engineer.
- P2 (feature broken for one customer): 4 business hours.
- P3 (question or minor issue): 1 business day.

Always reply in the customer's language when we have it (English, Icelandic, German). Close a ticket only after the customer confirms or after 5 days of silence. Refund requests go through the refund process; support may approve refunds up to ISK 30,000 on its own.
