# Hveravellir Hot Springs: onboarding

Went live on Skerry on 7 October 2024. Three-person team running a geothermal spa and a small cafe at the edge of the highlands, open only May to October. Setup took 6 working days instead of the usual 10, thanks to the new onboarding wizard prototype.

Product choices: single resource calendar, booking widget in Icelandic and English, Paylink for payment. Owner and main contact is Þorvaldur Hrafnsson (no relation to the company). They are one of eight pilot customers for the Skúmur beta.
