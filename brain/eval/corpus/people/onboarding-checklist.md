# New starter checklist

**Before day one:** People team orders the laptop, creates accounts, and tells the buddy.

**Day one:** Arrive 09:00 at reception. Pick up the laptop and access card from Davíð. Set up 1Password and hardware key. Lunch with the team.

**Week one:** Read the security basics, complete the data protection e-learning (45 minutes), shadow two customer calls, meet the CEO for coffee.

**Day 30:** Check-in with manager and the People team.
**Day 90:** Probation review. The home-office grant must be claimed before this.
