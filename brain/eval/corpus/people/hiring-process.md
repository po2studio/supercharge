# Hiring process

Four stages: a 30-minute call with People, a technical or craft interview of 60 minutes, a 90-minute pairing or case session with two team members, and a final conversation with the head of function. We aim to give a decision within 5 working days of the last interview. References are checked after the verbal offer. Every candidate gets written feedback on request. Interviewers must complete the unconscious-bias module once a year.
