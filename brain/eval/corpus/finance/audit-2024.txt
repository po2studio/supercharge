Notes from the external audit close meeting held on 6 February 2025 with the auditors from Kjarni Endurskoðun: the audit of the 2024 financial statements found no material misstatements and the auditors will issue an unqualified opinion. Three management letter points were raised: first, the revenue cut-off for annual subscriptions invoiced in December needs a clearer policy, second, two fixed assets were still in the register after being donated, and third, the approval trail for three expense claims above ISK 150,000 was incomplete. Björn accepted all three and promised to fix them before the March close. The auditors praised the quality of the reconciliation of Paylink settlements. The audit fee was ISK 3.2 million, in line with budget. The statements will be signed by the board on 25 February and filed with the tax authority before the end of the month.
