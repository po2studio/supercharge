# Budget 2025

Approved by the board on 12 December 2024. All figures in ISK millions.

## Revenue

Target revenue is 980, up 23% on the 2024 outturn of 798. The assumptions: 214 operators growing to 250, an average price increase of 4% from 1 March, and the seasonal plan bringing in about 12. Euro-denominated revenue is 55% of the total.

## Costs

| Line | Budget |
|---|---|
| Salaries and benefits | 520 |
| Infrastructure and hosting | 54 |
| Office and facilities | 63 |
| Marketing | 38 |
| Project Skúmur (mobile app) | 38 |
| Travel and offsite | 22 |
| Professional services | 19 |
| Contingency (3%) | 29 |

Operating costs add up to 754; with the contingency the total is 783, leaving a planned EBITDA of about 197, a margin of 20%.

## Headcount

Growth from 40 to 46: three engineers, one support specialist, one designer and one customer-success manager. Average cost per hire including recruiting is ISK 1.1 million. Hiring is paced so that no more than two people join per quarter before April.

## Capital expenditure

Fit-out of the Fiskislóð office is capped at 28 and is not included in operating costs above. Laptop refresh: 14 devices at ISK 410,000 each.

## Risks and sensitivities

A 10% fall in the euro would reduce revenue by roughly 54. A delay in Project Puffin beyond Q2 would add around 6 in duplicate hosting costs. The budget assumes no price-related churn above 5%.

## Reporting

Björn presents actuals against budget monthly to management and quarterly to the board. Any variance above 8% in a line needs a written explanation. Department heads may reallocate up to 2 million between lines without approval.

## Quarterly phasing

Revenue is phased 19% in Q1, 24% in Q2, 36% in Q3 and 21% in Q4, reflecting the summer peak. Costs are flatter: 24%, 25%, 26% and 25%. Cash is therefore at its lowest point in March, when annual insurance, the audit fee and the first hosting commitment for the year are paid; the minimum forecast cash balance is ISK 205 million, comfortably above the 100 million floor set by the board.

## Pricing assumptions

The 4% list-price increase from 1 March does not apply to the entry plan, which stays at ISK 38,000 per month to protect small operators. The larger plan rises from ISK 112,000 to ISK 116,500 per month. Transaction fees through Paylink are passed through at cost plus 0.2 percentage points.

## Approval workflow

Spending above ISK 2 million not in the budget needs the CFO and CEO together. Spending above ISK 10 million needs the board. Annual software subscriptions are reviewed in September, and the People team keeps the training budget separately at ISK 150,000 per person.

## Notes on salaries

The salary line assumes an average increase of 5.5% from 1 July, in line with the collective agreement for the sector, plus the new education grant and the top-up for parental leave. Employer pension contribution is 11.5% of salary and is included.
