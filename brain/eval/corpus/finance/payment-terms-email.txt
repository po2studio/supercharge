From: Björn Þorsteinsson To: all account managers Subject: Payment terms reminder. Hi all, a quick reminder because I keep getting questions: our standard payment terms are net 30 from the invoice date for all operators. The only exception is Nordic Wanderers, who have net 45 written into their contract. If an invoice is overdue by more than 7 days, send the first reminder automatically through Payday; at 21 days the account manager calls, and at 45 days overdue we suspend the booking widget after warning the customer in writing. Late-payment interest is 1.2% per month, calculated daily, and we only waive it with my approval or Sigríður's. Please do not give any customer extended terms verbally, because it ends up creating disputes in the audit. For customers paying in euro, bank charges are on the payer, so make sure the invoice says OUR for the charge code. Finally, new customers must pay the first invoice before go-live if the annual value is under ISK 500,000. Thanks, Björn.
