# Spotting fake mail

Typical signs: urgent tone, sender domain that is almost ours (hrafn-travel.example instead of hrafn.example), unexpected invoices, requests to buy vouchers.

If a message seems to be an attempt to trick you, do not follow links or open attachments. Forward it as an attachment to phishing@hrafn.example and then delete it. IT replies within 2 hours on working days. If you already entered credentials, call the IT on-call number 555-0142 immediately and change your passphrase.
