# Laptop policy

Notebooks are replaced every 36 months. Engineers receive a 14-inch MacBook Pro with 32 GB RAM; everyone else gets a MacBook Air. Disks are encrypted with FileVault, enforced through MDM.

A lost or stolen device must be reported to security@hrafn.example within 2 hours of discovery, even outside office hours. IT will remotely wipe it. Returned devices are wiped and donated to a local school after 5 years.
