# Glossary

- **Skerry**: our booking platform.
- **Geysir**: the pricing engine.
- **Operator**: a customer business, such as a tour company.
- **Guest** or **traveller**: the end user making a booking.
- **P1/P2/P3**: support priority levels.
- **Soft lock**: the 15-minute hold on inventory during checkout.
- **Payday**: the expense and invoicing tool.
- **Hrafn**: raven, and our name.
