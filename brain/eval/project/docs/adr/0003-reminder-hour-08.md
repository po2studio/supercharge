# 0003: Reminders go out at 08:00

Status: superseded by [0004](0004-customer-chosen-hour.md)

## Context

Reminders need one send time, and early morning means they are read before the working day.

## Decision

Every reminder goes out at 08:00 Reykjavík time.

## Consequences

Customers in other time zones get their reminders at an odd hour.
