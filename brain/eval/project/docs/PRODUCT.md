# Selkie product

Selkie sends a first reminder three days after an invoice falls due, a firm second one after fourteen days, and a final notice after thirty. Each customer can pause reminders for a single invoice, for example while a dispute is open; a paused invoice stays paused until someone resumes it, and it never expires on its own.

A reminder is always written in the language of the invoice, and never goes out on a weekend or an Icelandic public holiday. Customers that pay before the reminder is due are skipped silently.
